Express · Montgomery · Overnight redesign gallery

36 futuristic layouts — pick winners when you wake

Six comps per major tool. Recommended starters outlined in teal. Direction: ink + signal lime Command Deck that still dials appointments one-handed. Spec: docs/superpowers/specs/2026-11-11-futuristic-redesign-layouts.md

Home — most useful command deck

Job: open the app and know what to do in under 3 seconds. Primary = next call or next stop.

Recommended

1 · Mission Pulse

Next-action hero + KPIs + smart widgets. Best urgency/overview balance.

2 · Orbital Command

Radial quick-launch around the active company. High novelty, some speed risk.

3 · Day Spine

Vertical Now → Midday → Close timeline. Strong narrative, longer scan.

4 · Glass Stack

Action / Pipeline / Intelligence layers. Premium; watch stack height.

5 · Route Ribbon

Car-day map ribbon + stops. Weaker for pure Office dialing.

6 · Focus Strip

Brutalist Now / Next / Later. Max clarity, less spectacle.

Today — dialer & Office/Car

Weinberg reasons + appointment asks with ← → and thumbs. Never invent numbers.

Recommended

1 · Dial Cockpit

Huge number, reason/ask carousels, thumbs. Appointment machine.

2 · Split Stage

Left reason / right ask. Great for coaching; denser on small phones.

3 · Queue Ribbon

Horizontal company chips above detail. Fast skip.

Car

4 · Car HUD

Navigate / Arrived, low text while driving.

5 · Card Stack

Swipe stack of call cards. Delightful, less precise.

6 · Weinberg Theater

Opener / reason / ask / objections stage. Coaching-heavy.

MPC — resume ↔ market

Zip-style fit first, then company actions.

Recommended

1 · Fit Leaderboard

Ranked Great/Good with skill chips — matches shipped product.

2 · Resume | Jobs

Split candidate vs matches. Desktop-strong.

3 · Match Radar

Concentric fit rings. Memorable, weaker for many companies.

4 · Dense Filters

Filter-first rail + compact rows. Power-user speed.

5 · Story Cards

Large narrative why cards for client talk-tracks.

6 · Compare Tray

Pin 2–3 companies to compare fit.

Accounts — book of record

Find fast → dossier. Never invent contacts.

Recommended

1 · Search First

Giant search + recent + badges. Classic and fast.

2 · Territory Lanes

Grouped by city for route thinking.

3 · Badge Rail

Jackpot / Missing / First-year filters.

4 · Dense Ledger

Spreadsheet energy for power users.

5 · Spotlight

One account hero + dossier peek.

6 · Activity Feed

Book as timeline of touches.

Q4 — close the day

Finish wraps, hit goals, no loose ends.

Recommended

1 · Checklist Focus

One wrap at a time with goals. Hard to misuse.

2 · Goal Rings

Visual progress for calls/visits.

3 · Card Queue

Swipe through open wraps.

4 · Day Ledger

Accounting-style day sheet.

5 · Two-pane Wrap

Form left, company context right.

6 · Close Celebration

When goals hit — brief + reward. Use sparingly.

Scout — hiring intelligence

Turn signals into dials/visits without drowning in boards.

Recommended

1 · Signal Feed

News-style hiring cards → Call/Add.

2 · Calendar Spine

14-day scout calendar planning.

3 · Leads Board

Kanban New / Working / Won.

4 · Today10 Focus

Only ten cards. Forces prioritization.

5 · Prospect Radar

Outside-book by distance/signal.

6 · Briefing Console

Morning intel + paste import.
